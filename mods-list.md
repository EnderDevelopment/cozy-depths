# Rainy Hearth mod list

All entries target **Fabric + Minecraft 1.21.1**. Open the project link and install the compatible release through the Modrinth App. Dependencies are intentionally listed separately when they are commonly useful, but the app should resolve the complete dependency tree.

## Performance, shaders, and core

- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) — common Fabric library required by many mods.
- [Sodium](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium) — fast client rendering.
- [Iris Shaders](https://modrinth.com/mod/iris) — shader loader that works with Sodium.
- [Lithium](https://modrinth.com/mod/lithium) — server/gameplay optimization.
- [FerriteCore](https://modrinth.com/mod/ferrite-core) — reduced memory use.
- [ImmediatelyFast](https://modrinth.com/mod/immediatelyfast) — faster GUI and rendering paths.
- [Entity Culling](https://modrinth.com/mod/entityculling) — avoids rendering hidden entities.

## Home atmosphere

- [AmbientSounds](https://modrinth.com/mod/ambientsounds) — biome, cave, and weather ambience.
- [Sound Physics Remastered](https://modrinth.com/mod/sound-physics-remastered) — reverb, muffling, and occlusion for rooms and caves.
- [Falling Leaves](https://modrinth.com/mod/fallingleaves) — small, calm seasonal visual detail.
- [Continuity](https://modrinth.com/mod/continuity) — connected textures for supported resource packs.

## Exploration, mining, and food

- [AppleSkin](https://modrinth.com/mod/appleskin) — hunger and saturation information.
- [Traveler's Backpack](https://modrinth.com/mod/travelersbackpack) — portable storage and utility tools.
- [Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade) — readable block and entity information overlay.
- [Waystones](https://modrinth.com/mod/waystones) — simple travel between discovered locations.
- [Xaero's Minimap](https://modrinth.com/mod/xaeros-minimap) — configurable minimap.
- [Xaero's World Map](https://modrinth.com/mod/xaeros-world-map) — full-screen exploration map.
- [EMI](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi) — recipe and usage viewer.

## Gentle life and vanilla-style variety

- [Friends & Foes](https://modrinth.com/mod/friends-and-foes) — vanilla-style forgotten mobs and variants.
- [Naturalist](https://modrinth.com/mod/naturalist) — wildlife and peaceful nature encounters.

## Easy quality of life and light multiplayer

- [Mouse Tweaks](https://modrinth.com/mod/mouse-tweaks) — quicker inventory management.
- [Shulker Box Tooltip](https://modrinth.com/mod/shulkerboxtooltip) — inspect container contents without placing them.
- [Inventory Profiles Next](https://modrinth.com/mod/inventory-profiles-next) — optional sorting and inventory profiles.
- [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-voice-chat) — proximity voice for servers; optional.

## Shader pack

- [Complementary Reimagined](https://modrinth.com/shader/complementary-reimagined) — the recommended shader pack. Start at Low/Medium on the RTX 4060 laptop, enable a capped frame rate, and increase quality only if temperatures and frame rate remain comfortable.

## Deliberate omissions

- No infinite-food exploit or food mod is included: AppleSkin plus ordinary food keeps survival understandable and balanced.
- No fullbright is included by default: it removes the reason to use torches and weakens the cozy cave atmosphere. If you personally need it, add a current Fabric 1.21.1 brightness mod through the launcher and check its project page for compatibility.
- No heavy technology tree is included. The pack's technical layer is storage, recipes, maps, travel, and performance rather than machines and automation.
